## 🕹️ About

Wanderburg is a minimalist medieval action roguelike about commanding a roaming castle on wheels. Drive through a dangerous world, devour villages and smaller fortresses, and defeat powerful enemy castles to grow your moving war machine. Build modular combinations of siege weapons, arcane machinery, vehicles, artifacts, and captains across successive runs.

Wanderburg is for players who enjoy fast, replayable roguelikes with creative builds, approachable controls, and chaotic top-down fortress battles.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🏰 **Castle on Wheels** — Pilot a mobile fortress through a medieval world of roaming strongholds.
- ⚔️ **Action Roguelike Combat** — Battle villages, enemy castles, and other threats in replayable runs.
- 🛠️ **Modular Arsenal** — Mount siege weapons, arcane towers, explosives, and other combat modules.
- 🌱 **Grow Through Destruction** — Consume settlements and smaller fortresses to expand your castle.
- 🔓 **Run-Based Progression** — Unlock vehicles, artifacts, captains, and new strategic possibilities between runs.
- 🎮 **Flexible Controls** — Play with mouse and keyboard or a controller.
